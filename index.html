How this timer works

What is a pomodoro timer?

It's a timer that helps you work in short, focused stretches: about 25 minutes on one thing, then a 5 minute break, with a longer break after a few rounds. Knowing a break is always coming makes it easier to start and easier to stay put.

It's especially good if you procrastinate, get pulled off task easily, lose track of time, or tend to work until you're fried. Many people with ADHD find it helpful. The breaks keep your energy steady, and each finished block is a small win you can see.

Learn more about the Pomodoro Technique

How to use this timer

Tap Start focus block and get to work.
When time's up, take a break. It starts by itself.
Break over? Tap I'm back to keep going.

Want to walk away? Keep the sound on. It'll tell you when to take a break and when to come back.

Like to watch it? Keep this screen open and watch the circle fill up.

Only have a set amount of time? Type it in under How much time do you have to work? and tap Plan my time. It figures out the rest.

Make it fit you

25 minutes feels like a lot? That's normal. Start smaller.

Open Timer settings and change Focus block length to something that feels easy, like 10 minutes.
When that starts to feel easy, add a few minutes. Keep building up as your focus gets stronger.
You can change your breaks the same way.

Short is fine. Finishing a 10 minute block beats quitting a 25 minute one.
